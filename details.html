The 2016Q3–2019Q4 board is the stricter fully published-live official comparison. The 2021–present board is the decision-relevant post-COVID record. The modern NY Fed publication history is shorter, so common support contracts when it is included. Component results use the selected fixed-gold history and are therefore pseudo real-time; the earlier pure true-vintage run remains banked separately.

Composite. The ⅔ Direct / ⅓ Components point blend was fixed before the final combined evaluation. The Components model's distinct errors improved the Direct forecast materially, especially post-COVID; the better post-COVID 50/50 sensitivity was not selected ex post. The Composite has no claimed predictive density or news decomposition.

Retired history retained: Original DFM (retired), Bayesian DFM v1 (retired), LASSO bridge (retired), Chronos-2 (retired), Legacy equal-weight ensemble (retired), BISTRO (retired).
