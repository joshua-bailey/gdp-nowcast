Backtest 2 · Multi-model true-vintage horse-race

A second, different backtest: instead of validating the production DFM alone, this one pits four independent models and their equal-weight ensemble against each other on identical real-time data, to test whether combining models beats any single one.

The equal-weight ensemble is the most accurate in both regimes. The ensemble's edge over the DFM is statistically significant post-GFC (Diebold-Mariano p=0.10) and directional post-COVID (p=0.18, a short 16-quarter sample).

The ensemble wins because its members make largely independent errors (low/negative correlations above), so averaging cancels much of each one's noise.
