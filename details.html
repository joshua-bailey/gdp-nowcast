Backtest 2 · Multi-model true-vintage backtest (beta models)

A second, different backtest: instead of validating the production DFM alone, this one pits four independent models and their equal-weight ensemble against each other on identical real-time data, to test whether combining models beats any single one. A standalone research piece over two eras: post-GFC (2010Q4–2019Q4, a fixed pre-COVID window) and post-COVID (2021Q1 onward, the full record, which extends as each new quarter graduates). The rolling 8-quarter zoom-in is the Live Horserace.

The advance→third revision is itself ~0.78pp RMS over the 19 scored post-COVID quarters, so the gap between the two views is essentially the irreducible noise in the advance number, not model error. (Quarters whose third estimate is not yet published are excluded from the 'vs final' view, so its post-COVID sample is smaller, through 2025Q4.) Post-GFC is a fixed pre-COVID window; post-COVID extends as the Live Horserace scores each graduated quarter.

Low/negative error correlations (above) are why combining helps: the members make largely independent errors, so averaging cancels much of each one's noise.

Live Horserace · which model is winning right now

A live, rolling scoreboard over the most recent 8 quarters (2024Q2 to 2026Q1). It advances automatically: each time a new advance GDP print lands, the newest quarter is scored and the oldest drops off. It tracks how the production DFM and the beta models (Tuned Bayesian DFM, LASSO bridge, Chronos-2) and their equal-weight ensemble are doing in near real time. Models are scored on exactly the same basis as the static "Backtest 2" research (details page): true point-in-time vintages, the final pre-release forecast, vs the advance GDP print. CRPS generalises MAE to the whole predictive distribution (lower is better); coverage compares the realised hit-rate to the band’s nominal level. DFM, Tuned Bayesian DFM and the ensemble join once their bands are logged.

Scored on the day-before-release ("final") horizon against the advance GDP estimate. Diebold-Mariano p is the test that a model's accuracy differs from the DFM's; small samples make it conservative. Each model holds out the target quarter's GDP, so the comparison is leakage-free.
